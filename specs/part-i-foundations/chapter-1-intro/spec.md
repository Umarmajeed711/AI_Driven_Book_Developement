# Chapter Specification: Introduction to Physical AI

**Chapter Number**: Part I - Chapter 1  
**Feature Branch**: `ch001-intro-physical-ai`  
**Created**: 2025-12-22  
**Status**: Draft

## 1. Chapter Purpose

Introduce Physical AI and embodied intelligence as a distinct paradigm from digital AI.

## 2. Learning Objectives (Measurable)

Upon completing this chapter, a learner must be able to:
- [LO-01]: Define Physical AI and embodied intelligence.
- [LO-02]: Explain why physical constraints matter in AI systems.
- [LO-03]: Compare and contrast digital AI vs Physical AI systems.

## 3. Prerequisite Knowledge

None

## 4. Conceptual Sections (Theory)

- [CON-01]: **Defining Physical AI**: What it is, its scope, and why it's distinct.
  - Key terms: Physical AI, Embodied Intelligence, Embodied Cognition.
- [CON-02]: **Digital AI vs. Physical AI**: Core differences in approach, problems, and solutions.
  - Key terms: Digital AI, Symbolic AI, Connectionist AI, Physical Constraints, Embodiment.
- [CON-03]: **The Sense-Plan-Act Loop**: Basic robotic control architecture.
  - Key terms: Sensors, Actuators, Planning, Execution.
- [CON-04]: **Physical Constraints and Systems Thinking**: How real-world limitations influence AI design.
  - Key terms: Noise, Latency, Uncertainty, System Integration.

## 5. Practical Sections (Hands-On)

None (This chapter is theory-focused and introduces foundational concepts).

## 6. Physical-World Constraints Addressed

This chapter introduces the fundamental concepts of how physical-world constraints impact AI systems, without diving into implementation details.
- [PWC-01]: Sensor noise and its implications.
- [PWC-02]: Actuation delays and their effects on control.
- [PWC-03]: Environmental uncertainty and its challenge to planning.

## 7. Tools Introduced (with Justification)

None (This chapter is theory-focused and does not introduce specific tools).

## 8. Reproducibility Requirements

None (No hands-on content in this chapter).

## 9. Exercises and Assessment Criteria

- [EX-01]: **Conceptual Definitions**: Provide definitions for key terms (e.g., Physical AI, Embodied Intelligence).
  - Assessment Criteria: Accuracy and completeness of definitions.
- [EX-02]: **Comparative Analysis**: Write a short essay comparing a given digital AI system with a hypothetical Physical AI system addressing the same problem.
  - Assessment Criteria: Identification of key differences, understanding of constraints, clarity of argument.
- [EX-03]: **Problem Identification**: Given a real-world robotics scenario, identify potential physical constraints and their impact.
  - Assessment Criteria: Ability to correctly identify and explain relevant physical constraints.
